The modelling concepts and examples in this series are adapted from Blair Archibald, Muffy Calder, and Michele Sevegnani, Practical Modelling with Bigraphs, published in Formal Aspects of Computing (see [\[1\]](#ref1)), and are reformulated here as hands-on exercises with corresponding implementations using the Bigraph Framework.

## I Modelling Structure

1. [Place Graphs, Sites and Operators: Model hierarchical structure and unspecified or variable contents.](../exercises/place-graphs)
2. Link Graphs and Interfaces: Model connectivity, while distinguishing open interfaces from internal connections.
3. Combining Place and Link Structure: Construct complete bigraphs by integrating spatial hierarchy, connectivity and interaction.

## II Modelling Behaviour

4. Reaction Rules: describing state transformations
   1. Context and Sites in Rules: preserving unspecified structure during rewriting
   2. Parameterized Rules: defining reusable families of reactions
5. Controlled Rewriting: constraining when and where reactions may occur
6. Tracking Entities over Time: identifying and following nodes across reactions
